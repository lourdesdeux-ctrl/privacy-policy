Privacy Policy

Kokokara: Mood & Self-Care Log
Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the app "Kokokara: Mood & Self-Care Log" (the "App") handles your information.

1. Information stored by the App

The App lets you record the following information:

  • Your mood records and quick daily notes
  • Care items you register (name, category, usage, shop, purchase date, price and memo)
  • Your care records (date and time)
  • App settings (reminder times, dark mode)

All of this information is saved only on your own phone. The App does not require an account, and we do not collect, receive or store this information on any server. Information related to the ads shown in the App is described separately in "4. Advertising."

2. Sharing with third parties

We do not share, sell or transfer the records you enter in the App to any third party. However, to show ads, the information described in "4. Advertising" is sent to advertising providers such as Google AdMob.

3. Reminders (notifications)

Reminders are created and shown on your phone only. No internet connection is used for reminders. You can turn them off at any time in the App's settings or in your phone's settings.

4. Advertising

To keep the App free, it shows ads provided by Google AdMob, an advertising service operated by Google LLC. Ads appear at the bottom of the screen.

To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect information such as:

  • Your advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Your IP address
  • Device information (such as model, operating system version, language and screen size)
  • Information about how ads are displayed and tapped

This information is handled by Google and its advertising partners in accordance with Google's privacy policy. The mood records, notes, care items and other content you enter in the App are never sent for advertising purposes. For details, please see Google's page on how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or other regions where it is required, you may see a screen asking for your consent regarding the use of information for ads. You can change your choice later.

You can turn off personalized ads or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (for example, under "Google" and then "Ads"). The location and name of these settings may differ depending on your device.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete all records at any time using "Delete all data" in the App's settings. Uninstalling the App also removes the data saved on your phone.

6. Not medical advice

The App is for personal self-care and note-taking. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Please follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback, please use this contact form.
