Privacy Policy

Kokokara: Mood & Self-Care Log
Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the app "Kokokara: Mood & Self-Care Log" (the "App") handles your information.

1. Information stored by the App

The App lets you record the following information:

  • Your mood records and quick daily notes
  • Care items you register (name, category, usage, shop, purchase date, price and memo)
  • Your care records (date and time)
  • App settings (reminder times, dark mode)

All of this information is saved only on your own phone. The App does not require an account, and we do not collect, receive or store this information on any server.

2. No sharing with third parties

Because your records stay on your phone, we do not share, sell or transfer them to any third party.

3. Reminders (notifications)

Reminders are created and shown on your phone only. No internet connection is used for reminders. You can turn them off at any time in the App's settings or in your phone's settings.

4. Advertising

The App currently shows no advertising. In the future, we may introduce advertising (for example, Google AdMob). If we do, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe what information the advertising service may collect, such as an advertising identifier.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete all records at any time using "Delete all data" in the App's settings. Uninstalling the App also removes the data saved on your phone.

6. Not medical advice

The App is for personal self-care and note-taking. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Please follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback, please use this contact form.
